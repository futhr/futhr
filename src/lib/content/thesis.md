Software in consequential domains needs durable state, explicit contracts, verification, and recovery beneath its interface. This portfolio builds platforms and open infrastructure on that foundation. Probabilistic systems can propose and interpret; **deterministic systems decide what runs** and record the outcome. Shared cores reduce repeated engineering and preserve customer control, while commercial products earn revenue through operation and domain outcomes.

*Bootstrapped by default, built for durable ownership.*
