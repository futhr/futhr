A capital-efficient portfolio of platforms and open infrastructure for domains where software decisions carry operational, financial, or legal consequences.

**The thesis:** trust, interoperability, and unit economics are architectural constraints, not features added after scale. Across the portfolio, probabilistic intelligence sits above a deterministic foundation of durable state, explicit contracts, verification, and recovery. The full loop is designed as one: domain model, runtime, product, distribution, and operating model. Shared open-source cores reduce repeated engineering work and preserve customer control; commercial platforms monetize operation, assurance, and domain outcomes.

*Bootstrapped by default, built for durable ownership.*
