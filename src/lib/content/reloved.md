Conventional marketplaces centralize inventory and buyer relationships even when goods and sellers are local. Reloved plans **autonomous second-hand shops** whose operators keep control of their catalogs and sales. The nonprofit Reloved Commons is designed to make eligible public listings discoverable across those shops without becoming the seller or owning transactions.

The first researched profile serves households; later profiles may support reuse organizations and professional shops under their own legal and commercial responsibilities. Shared identity and open infrastructure can make goods easier to find while keeping authority with the people operating each shop.

*Owned by the shop, found through the Commons.*
