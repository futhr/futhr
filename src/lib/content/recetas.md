Recipes and meal plans live in apps; timers, shopping lists, and kitchen displays live elsewhere. **Recetas** brings them into one household-owned record, connecting what is planned, what is in the pantry, and what is happening at the stove.

The design centres on Raspberry Pi hosting and Home Assistant integration for cooking sessions, timers, and hands-free controls. Nx offers a path to local food intelligence, while recipes and everyday kitchen workflows remain independent of models. The household owns the data and decides what automation may change.

*The home becomes the interface.*
