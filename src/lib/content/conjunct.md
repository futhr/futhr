Product information often stops at the catalogue while engineering decisions, quotes, delivery, and service move through separate systems. Conjunct plans a shared context for **physical products and the work around them**, so manufacturers, engineers, and operators can carry product knowledge from an initial specification into a checked configuration and a clear commercial commitment.

Public catalogues and private collaboration would share the same product context without exposing every participant's work. The aim is to keep decisions connected through fulfilment, inspection, and later care, while allowing each organisation to retain control of its own information and relationships.

*One product context, from specification to care.*
