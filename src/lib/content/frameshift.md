Digital art often arrives as a screen or cloud service rather than an object that behaves like framed artwork. Frameshift studies three still-image media—color e-paper, matte LCD, and low-resolution RGB matrix—under **one capability-driven protocol**. A local host prepares display-specific files and lets each frame retain its last verified image.

The research separates frame hardware from rendering and control, so power, mounting, and protocol choices can be tested independently of any one vendor.

*Digital art presented as an object, not another screen to manage.*
