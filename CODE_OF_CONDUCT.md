# Code of conduct

Participation in this repository must remain professional, constructive, and
safe. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not
accepted.

Discuss the work, explain disagreements with evidence, respect project scope, and
assume that written communication may cross language and cultural boundaries.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hi@futhr.io](mailto:hi@futhr.io). The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably
possible, subject to safety and legal obligations.
